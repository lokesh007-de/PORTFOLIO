## Brand & Style

This design system draws inspiration from bespoke literary monographs, physical print ateliers, and architectural journals. Designed for a software craftsperson and developer, it deliberately departs from cold terminal interfaces and neo-brutalist tech tropes, positioning engineering as thoughtful craft, deliberate architecture, and tactile composition.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Design-forward founders, creative technologists, boutique agencies, and architectural engineering teams seeking senior talent with high aesthetic literacy.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Intimate, grounded, scholarly, and refined. The interface should feel like turning the heavy, matte pages of an archival publication printed on cotton rag paper, balancing warm tactile presence with uncompromising digital clarity.

### Visual Aesthetic & Philosophy
- **Style Direction:** Warm Editorial & Tactile Materiality.
- **Signature Motifs:** Generous asymmetrical margins, delicate single-pixel botanical hairline strokes, architectural arched image framing, warm sand card tiers, and textural restraint. Digital screens evoke the depth of physical print without skeuomorphic mimicry.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in natural pigments: raw terracotta, weathered field sage, warm calico paper, and wood-ash charcoal.

### Surface Tones & Canvases
- **Canvas Base:** `#F5EEE3` provides an unbleached linen ground. In layered regions or full-width page breaks, step into sunken paper `#EFE6D8`.
- **Card Surfaces:** `#EADECF` acts as elevated sand, establishing subtle visual groupings without stark white contrast.
- **Surface Accent Lines:** `#DDD0BE` for faint architectural dividers and botanical framing lines.

### Typographic Contrast
- **Primary Ink:** `#2B2520` (deep espresso charcoal), providing softer, richer legibility than synthetic black (`#000000`).
- **Muted Ink:** `#635B54` for metadata, dates, commit hashes, and deck copy.
- **Subtle Ink:** `#948A80` for captions, footnotes, and decorative section markers.

### Accents & Semantic Roles
- **Primary Accent (Terracotta / Burnt Clay - `#B5573A`):** Used for interactive focal points, selected states, featured project badges, and primary calls to action.
- **Secondary Accent (Field Sage - `#6B7A5A`):** Used for production statuses, engineering tags, live deployment indicators, and botanical accents.
- **Tertiary Accent (Warm Sand Clay - `#D8CAB8`):** Used for quiet state shifts, active pill fills, and soft surface framing.
- **Code Tint Ground:** `#ECE3D4` paired with `#382F2A` for embedded code blocks to preserve warm parchment continuity.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear editorial balance: expressive, transitional serif headlines pair with a geometric, legible sans-serif for reading and technical data.

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Imparts literature, editorial cadence, and weight. Larger headings should use an unforced optical weight (`400` or `500`) with tight line wrapping to mimic book titles. Italicized cuts are reserved for editorial sub-headers, quotes, and project case epigraphs.
- **Body & Utility (DM Sans):** Clear, proportioned, and calm. Set with open line heights (`1.65`–`1.875`) to encourage extended reading of architecture case studies and long-form technical write-ups.
- **Metadata & Technical Detail (JetBrains Mono):** Provides honest technical contrast for repository links, version tags, commit stamps, benchmarks, and line numbers.
- **Editorial Deck (`label-deck`):** Dedicated to italicized chapter leads, pull quotes, and project abstracts situated directly below major headlines.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an asymmetrical, wide-margin editorial grid that mirrors independent book design.

### Structural Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column asymmetric grid with `margin-desktop` (4.5rem) and `gutter-desktop` (3rem). Narrative articles sit within a 7-column offset spine, allowing 4 columns for sticky technical annotations, metadata, and botanical illustrations.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with `margin-tablet` (2.5rem) and `gutter-tablet` (2rem). Side notes collapse into inline sections.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4-column layout with `margin` (1.25rem) and `gutter` (1.5rem). Elements span full width with relaxed vertical breathing space.

### Spacing Cadence & Rhythm
- Spacing utilizes an intentional, non-linear progression to prevent visual crowding.
- Section boundaries employ generous pauses (`space-xl` and higher multiples) paired with subtle 1px divider lines (`#DDD0BE`) rather than heavy block dividers.
- Group related metadata using `space-xs` and `space-sm` to maintain clear grouping.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids glossy drop shadows, harsh dark casts, and synthetic blurs, relying instead on soft clay-style matte depth, unglazed terracotta textures, and layered paper sheets.

### Matte Surface Stacking
- **Ground Floor:** Canvas `#F5EEE3` with a persistent SVG microporous paper grain overlay at 3% opacity (`mix-blend-mode: multiply`).
- **Mid-Tier (Cards, Code Workbenches):** Shift upward via color values (`#EADECF`) accompanied by a subtle clay ambient drop:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(43, 37, 32, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(43, 37, 32, 0.03);`
- **Elevated Interactive (Modals, Hovered Projects, Floating Navbars):**
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(43, 37, 32, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(43, 37, 32, 0.04);`

### Border & Hairline Framework
- Cards and content regions use a delicate 1px boundary (`#DDD0BE` or `#D8CAB8`) to evoke the deckle edges of pressed rag paper.
- Hover states transition border colors toward terracotta (`#B5573A`) or sage (`#6B7A5A`) without shifting element dimensions.

## Shapes

The geometric vocabulary balances architectural precision with soft, organic curves.

### Architectural Arches & Curvature
- **Base Geometry:** A baseline roundedness of `1` (0.25rem default, 0.5rem on cards) keeps utilitarian interfaces crisp, deliberate, and book-bound.
- **Arched Feature Masks (`arch-top`):** Key portfolio screenshots, visual exhibits, and biographical portraits use an architectural round-top arch:
  `border-radius: 6rem 6rem 0.5rem 0.5rem;`
- **Botanical Pill Tags:** Filter tags and status chips use full pill geometry (`9999px`) to visually offset square-edged cards and code blocks.
- **Hairline Geometry:** In-line dividers feature botanical terminal loops or single-pixel dot ornaments at axis intersections.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid burnt terracotta (`#B5573A`) background with linen text (`#F5EEE3`). Slightly rounded corners (`0.375rem`), with a faint inset line: `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`. On hover, color deepens to `#A04B30`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent base enclosed by a 1px border (`#DDD0BE`), espresso text (`#2B2520`). On hover, background fills with sand (`#EADECF`) and borders transition to `#B5573A`.
- **Editorial Text Action:** Deep espresso link accompanied by an italicized serif label, terminating with a terracotta arrow glyph (`→`) that transitions 4px right on hover.

### Cards & Project Folios
- **Container:** Sand tint (`#EADECF`) over linen background, outlined by a 1px `#DDD0BE` perimeter line.
- **Cover Image / Exhibit:** Framed inside the arched mask (`border-radius: 6rem 6rem 0.375rem 0.375rem`), subtly recessed via a 1px inner border.
- **Content Block:** Editorial Playfair headline, muted summary deck in DM Sans, and monospace technology stamps across the bottom edge.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Tags (e.g., "Active Architecture", "Shipped"):** Sage ground tint (`rgba(107, 122, 90, 0.12)`), sage text (`#4F5C41`), encapsulated within a pill shape with a 1px hairline border (`rgba(107, 122, 90, 0.25)`).
- **Tech Stack Chips:** Sand ground (`#E2D5C3`), espresso label text in `JetBrains Mono`, zero drop shadow, 0.25rem radius.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Frame:** Sand background (`#EFE6D8`), 1px structural stroke in `#DDD0BE`. Text typed in `#2B2520`.
- **Focus State:** Stroke shifts to `#B5573A` with a soft 2px ambient outer glow (`rgba(181, 87, 58, 0.15)`). Placeholders styled in `#948A80` with an italicized serif posture.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Unchecked:** 1px perimeter border in `#DDD0BE`, warm linen interior (`#F5EEE3`).
- **Checked:** Solid terracotta fill (`#B5573A`) featuring a clean cream check mark. Radios adopt a center-nested terracotta dot.

### Lists & Case Indexes
- **Folio Index List:** Rows divided by 1px `#DDD0BE` lines. Each row contains an architectural chapter index (`01`, `02`) in `JetBrains Mono`, a Playfair project title, technical competencies in sage chips, and an interactive right-aligned release year.
- **Hover Motion:** Hovering a row reveals an arched image preview thumb without shifting adjacent line items.

### Code Display & Technical Workbenches
- Embedded code components use an inset matte parchment finish (`#ECE3D4`), left-aligned line numbers in `#948A80`, and warm-toned syntax highlighting (terracotta keywords, sage strings, and charcoal comments).
